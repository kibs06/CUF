# Summary of Chapter 1: The Problem and Its Scope  
**For Survey Questionnaire Development**  

## 1. Problem Statement  
- **Context:** Shoe retail businesses in Carcar City, Philippines, rely on manual operations (inventory, sales, customization tracking) that lead to inefficiencies, stock errors, and poor customer experience.  
- **Gap:** Lack of integrated systems for small footwear retailers combining inventory management, sales tracking, virtual fitting, and product customization.  
- **Impact:** Size mismatches, delayed order processing, unorganized customization requests, and lost sales opportunities.  

## 2. Proposed Solution  
**OTOP: A Footwear Retail Management System with Virtual Fitting Support**  
- **Core Features:**  
  - Centralized inventory management  
  - Sales and order tracking  
  - Virtual fitting via smartphone (AR-based preview)  
  - Digital product customization (color, material, special requests)  
  - Customer and seller portals (mobile app + admin web portal)  
- **Technology Stack:** Flutter (mobile), React (admin portal), Supabase (backend), AR for virtual fitting.  

## 3. Target Users  
| Role | Benefits |  
|------|----------|  
| **Customers** | Browse products, check sizes, virtual fitting, customize orders, track purchases. |  
| **Store Owner/Staff** | Manage inventory, process sales (POS), generate reports, handle custom orders. |  
| **Footwear Makers** | Receive clear customization details (design, size, due dates). |  

## 4. Objectives  
1. Investigate current retail operations of the partner store.  
2. Analyze requirements for system development.  
3. Design and develop OTOP with inventory, sales, virtual fitting, and customization modules.  
4. Evaluate using ISO 25010 (quality) and UTAUT (acceptability) frameworks.  
5. Deploy the system to the partner store.  

## 5. Scope & Limitations  
- **Scope:**  
  - Retail operations automation (inventory, sales, orders, customization).  
  - Customer-facing features (browsing, virtual fitting, customization requests).  
- **Limitations:**  
  - Virtual fitting accuracy depends on camera/lighting/device.  
  - Customization limited to store-approved options.  
  - Excludes internet connectivity, device performance, and store policy changes.  

## 6. Survey Questionnaire Focus Areas  
Based on Chapter 1, the questionnaire should assess:  
1. **System Usability** (ISO 25010): Ease of learning, navigation, error prevention.  
2. **User Acceptance** (UTAUT): Performance expectancy, effort expectancy, social influence, facilitating conditions.  
3. **Feature Relevance:** Importance of inventory management, virtual fitting, customization, and POS integration.  
4. **Impact on Operations:** Expected improvements in efficiency, customer satisfaction, and sales tracking.
